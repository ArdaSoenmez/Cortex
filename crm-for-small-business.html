FAQ

Frequently asked questions
about Vekto CRM.

What is a CRM for small business?

A CRM for small business is software designed to help small and medium-sized companies manage customer relationships, track projects, create proposals, and send invoices — all in one place. Unlike enterprise CRMs, it's affordable, quick to set up (<2 minutes for Vekto), and optimised for small teams rather than large corporations.

How much does Vekto CRM cost?

Vekto CRM offers three plans: Starter (free forever) with basic CRM features, Pro at $99/month with unlimited projects, invoices, and the AI Agent, and Scale at $249/month for larger teams up to 15 users. All paid plans include a 7-day free trial. No credit card required for the Starter plan.

Is Vekto CRM GDPR compliant?

Yes, fully. All data is stored exclusively on EU servers. Vekto CRM is GDPR-compliant by design: no user tracking for advertising, no data sold to third parties, full data export on request, and right-to-erasure supported. This makes it the ideal choice for European businesses that need strict data privacy compliance.

Does Vekto CRM support multiple languages?

Yes. Vekto CRM fully supports three languages: English, German (Deutsch), and Turkish (Türkçe). The entire interface, including navigation, forms, and generated documents, is available in all three languages. Users can switch languages at any time in account settings. This is unique among affordable CRM tools in the EMEA market.

Is there a free version?

Yes — the Starter plan is free forever. It includes customer management, up to 3 active projects, 5 offers per month, and PDF export. No credit card, no trial period, no automatic upgrade. You can use it as long as you want and upgrade to Pro when you need more capacity or the AI Agent.

How is Vekto CRM different from HubSpot?

Vekto CRM differs from HubSpot in three key ways: (1) Built for small business from day one — not an enterprise tool scaled down with complexity still intact; (2) Native invoicing and PDF generation that HubSpot doesn't include at any price tier; (3) Fully EU-hosted and multilingual (EN/DE/TR) while HubSpot stores data on US servers and lacks German/Turkish localisation. Vekto also starts at $0 vs HubSpot's $45/month minimum for paid features.
